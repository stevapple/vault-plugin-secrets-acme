# Changelog

Releases from `v0.4.0` onwards are described on the
[releases page](https://github.com/stevapple/vault-plugin-secrets-acme/releases),
which is written by goreleaser from the commits in each tag. There is no
hand-maintained changelog: keeping one alongside generated release notes means
writing every change twice, and the file this replaces had gone three years and
several releases without an entry.

Entries for `0.0.1` through `0.0.9` belonged to
[remilapeyre/vault-acme][upstream], where this engine was written. They are kept
in that repository's history rather than copied here, since nothing after
`0.0.9` was ever recorded and a partial record is worse than a pointer to a
complete one.

[upstream]: https://github.com/remilapeyre/vault-acme
